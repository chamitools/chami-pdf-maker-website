Frequently Asked Questions

How do I compress a file to 100KB?

Upload your image or PDF, pick Under 100 KB, then tap Compress to KB. You will see the before and after size before you download.

What if my file cannot reach 100KB?

Some files cannot go that small. In that case the tool gives you the smallest possible file and tells you its exact size, so you know where you stand.

Does compressing a PDF change the text?

For PDFs, each page is rebuilt as an image at a lower quality. The text still looks the same, but it is no longer selectable or searchable in the compressed file.

Are my files uploaded to a server?

No. Compression runs entirely in your browser. Your files never leave your device.
