Compress PDF

Make your PDF smaller for email and WhatsApp — free, no signup.

+ Drop a PDF here, or tap to choose One PDF at a time

How it works
  1. Add your PDF — drop a PDF file in, or tap to pick one.
  2. Pick a quality — High keeps the sharpest pages. Medium is a good balance. Low gives the smallest file.
  3. Download the smaller PDF — tap Compress PDF, then Preview or Download. You will see the before and after size first.

Please note: this tool rebuilds each page as an image at the quality you choose. Scanned and image-heavy PDFs shrink the most. Text in the compressed file looks the same but is no longer selectable or searchable. If your PDF is mostly text, it is already small and will shrink only a little.

How do I compress a PDF?

Upload your PDF, pick a quality — High, Medium or Low — then tap Compress PDF and download the smaller file.

Can I compress a PDF to 100KB?

It depends on the file. Scanned and image-heavy PDFs shrink a lot and often reach 100KB. Text-only PDFs are already small, so they shrink less.

Does compressing a PDF change the text?

In this tool, each page is rebuilt as an image at the quality you choose. The text still looks the same, but it is no longer selectable or searchable in the compressed file.

Are my PDFs uploaded to a server?

No. Compression runs entirely in your browser. Your files never leave your device.
