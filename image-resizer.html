Frequently Asked Questions

How do I resize an image?

Upload your image, enter a new width and height, choose a percent, or pick a preset like Instagram post or Passport, then tap Resize Image and download.

Can I reduce an image to an exact KB size?

Yes. Enter a target KB for JPG or WEBP output and the tool adjusts the quality until the file is close to that size.

Does resizing reduce image quality?

Making an image smaller always loses some detail, but this tool uses high-quality smoothing. Making an image larger than the original will look soft, so it is best to resize down.

Are my images uploaded to a server?

No. Resizing runs entirely in your browser. Your images never leave your device.
