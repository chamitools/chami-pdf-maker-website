Is Chami PDF Maker really free?

Yes. Every tool on this website is free to use, with no signup, no account and no watermark on your files.

Are my files uploaded to a server?

No. All processing happens in your own browser. Your files are never uploaded to any server, which also keeps the tools fast even when many people use the site at once.

Do the tools work on mobile?

Yes. The tools work on Android and iPhone browsers as well as on desktop. For the best experience on Android, get the Chami PDF Maker app.

Is there a file size limit?

There is no server limit, because files are processed on your device. Very large files are limited only by your device's memory.
