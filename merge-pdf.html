    How it works
    1. Add your PDFs — drop two or more PDF files in, or tap to pick them.
    2. Arrange the order — use Up and Down to put the files in the order you want them in the final document.
    3. Download the merged PDF — tap Merge PDFs, then Preview or Download.
    How do I merge PDF files?

    Add two or more PDF files, arrange them in the order you want with Up and Down, then tap Merge PDFs and download the combined file.

    Does merging PDFs reduce quality?

    No. Pages are copied exactly as they are, so text and images keep their original quality.

    How many PDFs can I merge at once?

    There is no set limit. You can merge as many PDFs as your device's memory can handle.

    Are my PDFs uploaded to a server?

    No. Merging runs entirely in your browser. Your files never leave your device.
