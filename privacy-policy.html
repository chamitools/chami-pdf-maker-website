Privacy Policy — Chami PDF Maker Website

Last updated: 17 November 2026. This policy covers the Chami PDF Maker website only. The Android app has its own policy: App Privacy Policy.

1. Your files never leave your device

All tools on this website run entirely in your web browser. When you choose a file, it is read and processed on your own device. We do not upload your files to any server, we do not store them, and we cannot see them.

2. No accounts, no personal data collected

There is no signup, no login and no account on this website. We do not ask for your name, email address or phone number, and we do not collect personal data from you.

3. Cookies and analytics

This website does not use tracking cookies and does not run analytics that identify you.

4. Advertising

This website may show ads from third-party ad providers such as Google AdSense. Ad providers may use cookies or similar technologies to show ads and to measure how ads perform. These cookies are set by the ad provider, not by us, and you can control or delete cookies in your browser settings. You can learn how Google uses data from sites that use its services at Google's partner-sites page.

5. Children's privacy

This website is for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.

7. Contact

Questions about this policy: chami.support@gmail.com
