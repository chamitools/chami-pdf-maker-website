Frequently Asked Questions

How do I split a PDF?

Upload your PDF, type the pages you want — for example 1-3, 5, 7-9 — then tap Split PDF and download the new file.

Can I get every page as a separate PDF?

Yes. Choose "Every page separately" and you will get a ZIP file with one PDF per page.

Does splitting a PDF change the quality?

No. The extracted pages are copied exactly, so quality stays the same.

Are my PDFs uploaded to a server?

No. Splitting runs entirely in your browser. Your files never leave your device.
